OBSERVED RESULT
---------------

Three-page Focus reflow:

The final production-equivalent three-page test exposed a separate reflow problem.
The saved anchor and both requested targets were:

    epubcfi(/6/4!/4/30/1:93)

But the final visible range reported by epub.js was:

    start: epubcfi(/6/4!/4/2/1:0)
    end:   epubcfi(/6/4!/4/20/3:11)

The requested anchor at node 30 is outside that final reported range, which confirms
that the visible rendition did not settle on the requested passage. The application
kept current.current at the correct anchor, but the visible EPUB surface moved to an
earlier page. This is separate from the silent-toggle bug and is not hidden or
described as passing in this report.

Queued Focus request:

The Focus request was queued while the saved ref still contained:

    epubcfi(/6/2!/4/1:0)

The page turn then settled and synchronously assigned:

    epubcfi(/6/4!/4/2/1:0)

Only after the position write settled was the queued Focus request applied. The
reflow anchor, resize target, display target, and final visible start CFI all used:

    epubcfi(/6/4!/4/2/1:0)

The queued Focus toggle therefore used the newly saved page instead of the stale
pre-turn page.
